02 Design Is the org built to act on it?

For: VP CS · Founder/CEO · first-time CS leaders

Most portfolios are balanced by account count, which doesn't take into account ARR size or risk. This quietly buries your best CSMs in accounts that would have renewed themselves. This is the coverage math, including your first tiered engagement model if every account is currently treated the same. It usually finds capacity you already have.

What you get
