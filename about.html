About

Twenty plus years of making technology and customers measurable.

I'm Dan Ahrens. I've spent my career in technology and working with customers. I've been a CSM, a leader of CSM teams, a product leader inside the platform vendor that defined the category, and for the last several years as the executive accountable for retention at global scale.

I've always been driven to make technology and customers measurable. For example, in 2012, at Kronos, our 32-person team was covering more than 1,200 customers with no CRM system or customer success platform to help. So I used the tools we had to build custom risk analytics and a simplified CRM - a decade before that kind of tooling became the standard. That builder mentality never left. Every role since has been some version of the same question: what do we actually know, how do we know it, and what is it worth?

What I do

I help B2B SaaS companies turn customer success into a function that runs on numbers: measurable health, deliberate coverage, and a team that acts on both. Sometimes that means building the function for the first time. Sometimes it means fixing one that stopped scaling or was focused on the wrong metrics. The tools are often the same; the sequence is repeatable.

In practice it's three things happening in sequence. 1) Assess and rebuild the measurement layer so the numbers mean something. 2) Redesign how coverage and engagement work so the team can act on those numbers. 3) Apply automation and AI so a smaller team covers more ground than it could before.
